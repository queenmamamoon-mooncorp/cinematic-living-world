# Design recipe
Use a calm, luminous world with intentional motion.
- Color: midnight shadows, emerald and violet midtones, rose-gold highlights; adapt to the user's palette. Protect skin and caption contrast.
- Light: one believable main source; let water and crystal reflections echo it.
- Depth: keep atmospheric background quieter than the presenter; foreground details can be sharper and move slightly faster when separate layers exist.
- Rhythm: 4–6-second glow/reflection cycles, 8–12-second breathing/sway cycles, staggered localized glints. Ease reversals and check loop seams.
- Visibility: evaluate at 360 × 640 display size. Make movement readable during one viewing without distracting from speech.
- Restraint: avoid indiscriminate particle blankets, global blur, fast flashes and constant camera movement.
- Integrity: keep original speech, timing and captions unless the user requested changes.

Flattened footage limits:
Water can be locally remapped where visible. Flowers can breathe through localized deformation. True blooms need separated petals. An obscured moon cannot be animated visibly; a visible emblem may receive a labeled halo. A moving rail highlight is a frame effect, not background animation.
